This project is a prototype for a story-driven action-adventure RPG set in a richly detailed ancient world inspired by true events. Players explore standalone historical scenarios or follow a cohesive single-player story mode, where moral choices, dynamic systems, and immersive storytelling bring the past to life.

- Rebuild sacred structures through collaborative construction systems
- Solve narrative-driven environmental puzzles
- Engage in melee and ranged combat against bandits, beasts, and hostile factions
- Navigate a living world shaped by a day-night cycle and dynamic NPC behavior
- Experience side quests, trading, stealth, and political intrigue
